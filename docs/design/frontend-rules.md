# Frontend direction, 2026-10-05

The user's latest visual restrictions take precedence over earlier concept screenshots and decorative treatments in the master plan.

## Opening page

- Use the three supplied sports videos, side by side in equal full-width segments.
- Order: tennis, basketball, running. Use deliberate athlete-centered crops.
- Place “Your work starts here.” and account controls below the opening section, reached by scrolling.
- Use muted loops, an accessible play/pause control, real poster frames, and reduced-motion support.
- Pause when the videos leave the viewport, the page loses focus, or the app/tab is hidden.
- Preserve the supplied originals; bundle smaller playback copies with provenance.

## Visual and copy rules

- Flat surfaces, square edges, warm neutral backgrounds, restrained ink and earth accents.
- No harsh gradients, pure-white backgrounds, rainbow palettes, neon colors, basic pastel palettes, purple/black pairing, radial orbs, or dot grids.
- No shadows, glass effects, rounded card treatments, bento layouts, three feature cards in a row, terminal-window decoration, or three pricing tiers.
- No Lucide icons, emojis, sparkle icons, animated arrows, hover animations, or decorative solid left stripes.
- Use Helvetica, Arial, or the platform sans serif. Do not introduce Inter, Geist, or Space Grotesk.
- No em dashes, invented testimonials, or “it's not x, it's y” copy.
- No skeleton loaders. Use a short loading status or actual poster images where appropriate.
- Do not add a product-demo section, terms section, or privacy-policy section to this welcome-page revision. The pre-existing account data explanation remains a separate route.
- The three video segments are actual supplied footage, with no fabricated analysis overlay or product-result claim.

## Implementation scope

The welcome page follows these rules. Shared light surfaces, elevation tokens, and Studio panel/control corner styles were also flattened for consistency. Legacy screens and unused components have not received a complete redesign in this iteration.
